SYSTEM INFORMATION

DATE CREATED: APRIL 2022

PROGRAMMING LANGUAGE USED: PHP, MySQL, JS

CPE E-LIBRARY is an academic resource repository system developed using PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, and HTML. This digital library is designed to store a variety of literary works related to the field of Computer Engineering, providing students with valuable additional resources for their studies.
